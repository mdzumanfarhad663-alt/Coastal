About us

Independent places.
Memorable stays.

Coastal Getaway Vacations brings together the independent hotels and suites in the Coastal Hospitality Group portfolio in one place to explore and book. Our collection includes affordable beach stays, coastal suites, and mountain escapes across the United States.

Every property has its own setting and personality. Our reservations and customer care agents can help you find the right fit and reserve your stay; the team at your property welcomes you when you arrive. It is a more personal way to discover a place to stay, with real people to call when you need them.

The company behind the collection

Part of Coastal Hospitality Group.

Coastal Getaway Vacations is the guest-facing booking home for properties owned and managed by Coastal Hospitality Group affiliate companies. Coastal Hospitality Group develops, acquires, and operates independent hospitality properties across the United States, with a focus on distinctive boutique places. Together, the group and its on-property teams connect guests with stays that retain their own character.
